# Fundraising Integrity

The rules behind a fundraising page that people can trust. This is general guidance for building the page. It is not legal advice. For anything unclear, the team should ask the charity, and check the fundraising rules for their state or territory.

## 1. The rules

**Donations go to the charity.** The donate button opens the charity's own official donation page. The team page never collects money, card details, bank details or account numbers, and never sits between a donor and the charity.

**One source for the link.** The donation URL comes from the user, copied from the charity's official page. If it is not supplied, leave `[NEEDED: official donation URL]` in place. The checker will not pass the site until it is filled in.

**Dated figures.** An amount raised is shown with an "as at" date and is only shown if the user supplied it. Progress bars show the real percentage of the real target. No placeholder numbers survive into the live page.

**No invented content.** Do not make up totals, sponsors, testimonials, quotes from the charity, awards, press coverage or numbers of supporters.

**Permission.**

- Sponsors: only listed with the sponsor's agreement.
- Charity name: saying "supporting [Charity]" is normal. Using the charity's logo, colours or style needs the charity's permission, so ask first.
- People in photos: ask before publishing photos of identifiable people. Take extra care with children.

**Say who is who.** The footer states who runs the page and that donations go directly to the charity. Use "independent" or "official" only if the user confirms that is accurate.

**Careful claims.** Do not state that "100% of donations go to charity", that gifts are "tax deductible", or that the team is "authorised" or "registered" unless the user confirms it and can point to the charity's own statement.

**Privacy.** Publish only the contact details the user has chosen to publish. Do not publish home addresses, personal phone numbers or children's full names.

**Keep it current.** Update figures and dates after each milestone. After the event, say it has finished, give the final dated total, and thank donors.

## 2. Safe wording

Use these as starting points and adjust to the facts.

| Purpose | Example |
|---|---|
| Who runs the page | "This page is run by [Team name], a team supporting [Charity]." |
| Where donations go | "Donations are made directly to [Charity] on its official donation page. We do not collect or handle donations." |
| Dated figure | "Raised so far: $[amount], as at [date]." |
| Sponsor thanks | "Thank you to our sponsors, who have agreed to be listed." |
| Cautious claim | "Please see [Charity]'s donation page for how donations are used." |

## 3. Wording to avoid

| Avoid | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| "We have raised $X!" with no date | Goes out of date and can mislead | Add "as at [date]" |
| "100% of every dollar goes to charity" | Needs the charity's confirmation | Point to the charity's own page |
| "Official [Charity] team" | Implies endorsement | "Team supporting [Charity]" unless confirmed |
| "Donate here" over a team payment form | Not allowed | Link to the charity's page |
| "Tax deductible" | Depends on the charity and the gift | Leave out, or quote the charity |
| Fake urgency such as "Only 2 days left!" when untrue | Misleading | Use the real end date |

## 4. Questions to ask the charity

- Is there an official donation page for our team, and what is its link?
- May we use your name and logo on our page, and how must they appear?
- Is there wording you want us to use or avoid?
- Do you have fundraising guidelines or approval steps for teams?
- Is there anything we need to register or declare for our state or territory?
- How should we describe where the money goes?

## 5. Quick check before going live

- [ ] The donate link opens the charity's official page
- [ ] Every figure has an "as at" date and was supplied by the team
- [ ] Every sponsor agreed to be listed
- [ ] Every person in a photo agreed, and children are handled carefully
- [ ] The footer notice is accurate
- [ ] No claim about tax or 100% unless the charity confirms
- [ ] No placeholder text is left on the page
- [ ] The team has read the page from top to bottom on a phone
