# Project handover and checks

## Portable record
- Working title / type / audience / reader outcome
- Target length / language / voice / publishing destination
- Current stage and last approved checkpoint
- Approved outline with chapter word budgets
- Draft chapters and their status
- Character, setting, chronology or terminology register
- Sources: claim | source title | URL | date | verification status
- Artwork register: asset | origin | permission status | intended placement
- Unresolved questions, unsupported claims and decisions required
- Next action and exact inputs needed

## Review gates
Brief: clear audience and useful outcome; realistic scope.
Research: supplied evidence distinguished from assumptions; links actually verified when claimed.
Outline: each chapter contributes; no unnecessary overlap; word budgets total correctly.
Draft: follows the approved scope; no invented facts, references or quotes.
Edit: author voice preserved; internal contradictions and repetition identified.
Artwork: recurring character details retained; legibility and rights checks recorded.
Export: contents links, headings, image descriptions, page breaks and typography checked in target software.
Publication: current destination rules checked; metadata accurate; AI use recorded; author approves final files.

## Adapting by book type
Fiction: track character motivations, point of view, tense, chronology and unresolved plot threads.
Non-fiction: separate evidence, interpretation and advice; include actionable examples.
Children's: set reader age, reading level and illustration rhythm; avoid arbitrary age suitability claims.
Workbook: include clear instructions, space to respond and answer notes where appropriate.
Travel journal: distinguish recorded experience from invented narrative; protect other people's private information.
