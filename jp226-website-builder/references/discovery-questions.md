# Discovery Questions

A question bank for defining a website. Do not ask everything. Pick the few that change the design, and offer defaults for the rest.

## Contents

1. Purpose
2. Audience
3. The one action
4. Content and pages
5. Look and feel
6. Features
7. Practical details
8. Defaults to offer when the user is unsure

## 1. Purpose

- In one sentence, what is this site for?
- What happens if the site works well? (more calls, more bookings, fewer questions, more sign-ups)
- Is there an existing site, social page or flyer to start from?
- Is it a permanent site, or for an event, a campaign or a launch?

## 2. Audience

- Who will visit? (customers, members, employers, locals, fans)
- What are they trying to do when they arrive?
- What do they worry about or need to be reassured on?
- Do most visitors use phones? (Assume yes unless told otherwise.)

## 3. The one action

Pick one main action. Everything else supports it.

| Action | Typical page feel |
|---|---|
| Call or visit | Big phone number, address, hours, map link |
| Book or enquire | Short pitch, what is included, clear contact route |
| Buy | Product list, prices, link to the shop or payment page |
| Learn | Clear headings, readable text, contents list |
| Follow or sign up | One short form or link, plus a reason to act |
| Be impressed | Strong visuals, selected work, short bio |

## 4. Content and pages

- What pages or sections do you need? (Home, About, Services, Gallery, Prices, Contact, FAQ)
- What words do you already have? Please paste or attach them.
- What images do you have? Logo, product photos, team photos, work samples.
- What facts must appear? Business name, address, opening hours, phone, email, social links, ABN or licence number if relevant.
- Anything you must not say or show?

## 5. Look and feel

- Three words for the feel. (Calm, bold, friendly, premium, playful, serious, handmade)
- Any brand colours or a logo to match?
- Sites you like, and what you like about them. Sites you dislike.
- Light, dark, or both?
- Tone of voice: formal, friendly, funny, plain and direct?

## 6. Features

| Feature | Note |
|---|---|
| Contact details | Always possible. Uses `mailto:` and `tel:` links. |
| Contact form | Needs a form service. Ask which, or use contact links instead. |
| Gallery | Needs the user's images. |
| Map | A link to a map is simple. An embedded map needs a provider. |
| Shop or payments | Link to a payment or shop service. Cannot be built into a static page. |
| Booking | Link to or embed a booking service. |
| Blog or news | Possible as a few static pages. Frequent posting needs a content system. |
| Login or members area | Needs a server. Explain the limit. |
| Newsletter sign-up | Needs an email service. Ask which. |

## 7. Practical details

- Do you have a domain name? Where is it registered?
- Do you have hosting, or do you need a place to put the files?
- When do you need it by?
- Who will update the site afterwards, and how often?
- Any rules to follow? (privacy policy, accessibility, industry rules)

## 8. Defaults to offer when the user is unsure

| Topic | Default |
|---|---|
| Pages | One page with sections: intro, what you offer, about, contact |
| Action | Contact by phone or email |
| Feel | Clean, friendly, plenty of white space |
| Colours | One accent colour on a neutral background, with a dark mode |
| Fonts | System fonts |
| Form | Contact links, not a form |
| Images | Labelled stand-ins until real ones arrive |

Say which defaults you chose in the brief so the user can change them.
